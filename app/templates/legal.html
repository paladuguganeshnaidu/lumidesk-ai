What we process

When you sign in, LumiDesk receives your verified Google account identifier, name, email address and profile image. The application stores account details, role, last sign-in time, basic usage events and report metadata such as the project title and page count.

AI features

Your learning questions and project instructions are sent to NVIDIA's hosted Nemotron API to produce responses. For report creation, the PDF is processed in memory. The application sends the new project details and limited layout cues (such as page dimensions, text-area sizes and short structural headings) to the AI service. It is designed not to send the reference report's body paragraphs for rewriting.

Uploaded files

Uploaded reference PDFs and generated report files are not intentionally retained on LumiDesk's server; the generated PDF is returned to your browser. Do not upload confidential, sensitive or third-party material without permission. The application stores report metadata and usage records.

Who can see account data

Authorized administrators can view student names, verified email addresses, account status and report metadata in the admin console. Administrators cannot use the console to read your uploaded PDF because files are not stored there.

Cookies and hosting

LumiDesk uses a signed session cookie to keep you signed in and protect state-changing requests. The service is hosted by Render. On Render Free, local files and SQLite records can be lost during restarts, sleep or redeployments. For a real institutional deployment, use durable managed storage and publish a retention schedule.

Third-party services

Google provides authentication, NVIDIA provides AI inference, and Render provides hosting. Their own privacy terms apply to the data they process. Review those policies before using the service with a class.

Your choices

Do not use the service if you do not agree to this processing. You can sign out at any time. For account deletion or questions, contact the service administrator. A formal deletion workflow and institutional data-processing agreement should be added before school-wide deployment.

Educational use

LumiDesk is an AI-assisted learning and drafting tool. It is not a teacher, examiner, institution or source of official academic approval. AI outputs can be incomplete or incorrect.

Original work and permissions

Use only PDF templates, logos and artwork that you own or are authorized to reuse. The Report Studio is intended to create original content based on your own project details. It must not be used to copy another student's research, fabricate experiments, invent references, forge signatures or misrepresent faculty approval.

Review before submission

You are responsible for checking generated explanations, calculations, citations, code, report content and formatting. Have an authorized faculty member complete and sign approval pages. A downloaded PDF is a draft, not a verified institutional submission.

Acceptable use

Availability
